Base Colours

All opherns have a base colour inherited from their parents which may be passed on to their descendants.  Base colours are one solid colour picked from or chosen from tones close to the sliders included in the section below.

Guidelines

  • Must be one flat colour without nuances before the application of markings.
  • Base colours do not have to be picked directly from the sliders within reason, as long as it remains clear which base your ophern has.  There may be some grey zone between bases like Junebug and Violet, but do take care to ensure that your ophern’s base tone is easy to read.
    • While you may lighten or darken your base tones, they should not read as white or black unless your character has the Bone/Darkling base, or the Melanistic or Albino mutations

Plentiful Bases

Mouse (Mse) - brown tones.

Pyrite (Pyr) - gold & pale yellow/orange.

Verdant (Vrd) - green tones.

Common Bases

Sapphire (Sph) - blue & blue-violet tones.

Junebug (Jne) - red & orange tones.

Uncommon Bases

Bone (Bne) - white & pastel tones.

Steel (Stl) - grey tones.

Rare Bases

Violet (Vlt) - pinks & purples.

Darkling (Drk) - black tones.
