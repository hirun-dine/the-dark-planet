The Dark Planet ARPG

The Dark Planet is a 16+ scifi ARPG where players take on the role of the survivors of a doomed colony ship who, after an unforseen delay, find themselves stranded on the mysterious planet Endymion and unable to contact the space empires that originally contracted their mission.

With remaining power and fuel sources a precious resource, the intelligent genetically-engineered ‘opherns’ carried on the ship—highly-adaptable beasts meant to serve as mounts for scouts during the initial colonisation of the planet—become an irreplaceably valuable resource.

Take on the mantle of the rider of one such ophern and aid in the colonists’ survival… or, perhaps, act in your own interests instead…
